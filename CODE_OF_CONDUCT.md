# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and welcoming. No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Give and accept feedback kindly; focus on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when unsure.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or PRs that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Amanbig) (private message or a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Amanbig/PhantomOps/security/advisories/new) if sensitive).
